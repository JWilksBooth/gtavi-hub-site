About GTA VI Hub

GTA VI Hub is an independent fan community and guide site for Grand Theft Auto VI, which releases on November 19, 2026 for PS5, PS5 Pro and Xbox Series X|S.

We are not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive, and we do not speak for them.

What we publish

Practical guides. Storage requirements, edition comparisons, platform choice, and launch preparation — written to answer the question a reader actually typed, with a real recommendation rather than a hedge. Several include interactive tools we built: a console storage calculator, a live countdown, and a crew-name generator.

Official news only. Our Discord news desk polls Rockstar's Newswire directly and mirrors confirmed announcements here. We do not publish leaks, rumours, datamined material, or AI-generated "leak" content — not as a stylistic preference, but as a firm editorial rule. Items that look leak-adjacent are held for human review and usually never run.

A community. The Discord server exists so players can find a crew to play with on launch day, matched by platform. It is free, all-ages appropriate, and moderated.

Editorial standards

  • We tell people not to buy things. Our setup guide's most common advice is "your current gear is fine." A recommendation only means something if we're willing to say when something isn't needed.
  • We label estimates as estimates. GTA 6's install size has never been published by Rockstar. Where we give planning figures, we say plainly that they are inferred from comparable releases, not official.
  • We disclose affiliate relationships on every page that carries one. See our affiliate disclosure.
  • We correct things. Guides carry an "updated" date, and we revise them as Rockstar confirms details.

Who runs it

GTA VI Hub is run by a small independent team — one operator plus automation — not a media company or content farm. Every guide on this site was written for it specifically; nothing is syndicated, spun, or scraped.
