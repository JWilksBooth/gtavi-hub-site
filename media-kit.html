Editorial standards

  • Official sources only. We publish no leaks, rumours, datamined material or AI-generated "leak" content. Items that look leak-adjacent are held for human review. This is enforced in software, not just policy.
  • Estimates are labelled. GTA 6's install size has never been published; where we give planning figures we say they are inferred.
  • We recommend not buying. Our setup guide's most common conclusion is that a reader's existing gear is fine.
  • Affiliate relationships are disclosed on every page carrying a link, and on every Discord deal post automatically. See our affiliate disclosure.
  • No prohibited categories, ever: no energy drinks, gambling, casino or betting, skin betting, or loot-box gambling. Our audience includes minors.

How products appear

Contextually, inside guides that answer a reader's question. A storage drive appears in the storage guide, after the free options have been covered, for the reader who genuinely needs more capacity. We do not run coupon pages, incentivised clicks, cashback, toolbars, or paid search on brand terms.
