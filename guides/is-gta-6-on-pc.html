Is GTA 6 coming to PC?

Short answer: not at launch, and Rockstar has announced nothing about a PC version.

GTA 6 releases November 19, 2026 on PS5, PS5 Pro and Xbox Series X|S. There is no announced PC release date, no storefront listing, and no published system requirements.

What Rockstar has actually said

Rockstar has confirmed the console platforms and the release date. It has not announced a PC version, given a window, or published PC specs. Every "GTA 6 PC requirements" article you have seen is speculation built on comparable games — sometimes reasonable, never official.

What the precedent suggests (and its limits)

Rockstar's recent releases have gone console-first, PC-later:

Game Console release PC release Gap
GTA V September 2013 April 2015 ~18 months
Red Dead Redemption 2 October 2018 November 2019 ~13 months

Applied naively to a November 2026 console launch, that pattern points somewhere in late 2027 to 2028. Treat this as pattern-matching, not a leak or a promise — the industry has changed, and Rockstar has never committed to repeating itself.

What this means for you

If you only have a PC: there is no launch-day option. Your realistic choices are buying a current-gen console or waiting an unknown period.

If you have a console: play at launch, and treat a future PC version as a bonus.

If you are buying a console for GTA 6: either current-gen family runs it. Decide on price, controller preference, and where your friends play — that last one matters most, because your crew has to be on your platform.

FAQ

Is GTA 6 on PC?
Not at launch. GTA 6 releases November 19, 2026 on PS5, PS5 Pro and Xbox Series X|S only. Rockstar has not announced a PC version, a PC release date, or PC system requirements.
When will GTA 6 come to PC?
Nobody knows, and anyone quoting a date is guessing. Rockstar's recent pattern is a console-first release with a PC version arriving later — GTA 5 took roughly a year and a half, Red Dead Redemption 2 about a year. That pattern suggests late 2027 or 2028, but it is precedent, not a promise.
Is GTA 6 coming to PS4 or Xbox One?
No. There is no last-gen release. The game is current-gen only.
Should I buy a PS5 for GTA 6?
If you want to play at launch and have no current-gen console, that is the only route. If you can wait an unknown number of months or years for a PC version that has not been announced, waiting costs nothing but patience.
