The factor that actually decides it

Where your friends play. Rockstar has not confirmed cross-play, so plan on your crew sharing a platform family. A slightly sharper image on a console where you play alone loses to a slightly softer one where your friends are.

If you have no existing group, our crew-finder is platform-aware — pick your platform, and /lfg only matches you with people on it.

Storage is the real differentiator

Fidelity differences between Series X and PS5 are likely to be marginal. The difference that will actually affect your launch night is space:

  • Series S owners have the tightest squeeze at ~364 GB usable. Plan to keep very little else installed, or budget for an expansion card.
  • Base PS5 and Series X owners are usually one or two game deletions away from fine.
  • PS5 Pro owners can stop reading.

Rockstar has not published performance-mode details. Expect the usual fidelity/performance split, with PS5 Pro getting the most headroom. Nobody outside Rockstar knows the numbers yet — treat published "leaked specs" as fiction.

FAQ

Is GTA 6 better on PS5 or Xbox Series X?
The PS5 and Xbox Series X are close enough in capability that any difference is likely to be minor. The bigger factor is which platform your friends play on, because your crew has to be on your platform.
Will GTA 6 run well on Xbox Series S?
It is a supported launch platform, so it will run — expect lower resolution and possibly reduced settings compared with Series X. Storage is the bigger practical concern: the Series S has roughly 364 GB usable.
Is GTA 6 cross-play between PS5 and Xbox?
Rockstar has not confirmed cross-play details. Until it does, plan for your crew to be on the same platform family.
Is the PS5 Pro worth it for GTA 6?
Only if you already wanted a Pro. A standard PS5 is a supported launch platform and will play the game fine.
