GTA 6 install size: how much storage do you actually need?

Short answer: free up about 150 GB and stop worrying about it.

Rockstar has not published an official install size, so anyone quoting an exact number is guessing. What we can do is bound the estimate sensibly and make sure you are not the person discovering the problem at midnight on November 19.

Usable figures account for system reserve. GTA 6's size is an estimate — no official number exists yet.

Why 150 GB is the number to plan around

Reference point Size
GTA V (current-gen, with GTA Online) ~95–115 GB, grew steadily over its life
Recent large open-world releases ~100–160 GB typical

That is planning guidance, not a spec. When Rockstar publishes the real figure our news desk will have it in minutes — that is literally what the Discord is for.

How to check your free space (2 minutes)

PS5: Settings → Storage → Games and Apps. Sort by size and be honest about the shooter you have not opened since March.

Xbox Series X|S: My games & apps → Manage → Storage devices.

Making room, cheapest first

1. Delete and re-download later — free. You own the game; you are only deleting the install. The catch is launch-week bandwidth, so do this in October, not on the 19th.

2. Archive to a cheap external USB drive — ~$40–70. Both consoles can store current-gen games on external USB storage. You cannot play them from there, but moving back is far faster than re-downloading. Best value if you rotate between a few big games.

3. Add real expansion storage — ~$80–160. - PS5 takes standard NVMe M.2 drives. Get 1 TB or larger, with a heatsink. A WD_BLACK SN850X-class NVMe is the common well-reviewed pick. - Xbox Series X|S uses the proprietary expansion card slot — the WD_BLACK C50 expansion card and Seagate's equivalent are the only real options.

Preload timing

Preorders typically unlock a preload a couple of days before release. Start it the moment it opens: a 150 GB download is an overnight job on most connections, and everyone else will be pulling it at the same time.

The one-line version

Free 150 GB by early November, preload the day it unlocks, and launch night is a non-event. Leave it to the 19th and you will spend day one watching a progress bar.

FAQ

How big is the GTA 6 download?
Rockstar has not published an official install size. Based on comparable open-world releases and GTA 5's growth over its lifetime, planning for 100 GB or more is sensible, and freeing around 150 GB gives you room for the game plus a day-one patch. Treat every number circulating online as an estimate until Rockstar confirms it.
Will GTA 6 fit on a base PS5?
A base PS5 has roughly 667 GB usable of its 825 GB. It will fit — the question is what else you have installed. Most people need to delete or archive one or two large games first.
Will GTA 6 fit on an Xbox Series S?
The Series S has about 364 GB usable, which makes it the tightest case. Expect to keep very little else installed, or add an official expansion card.
Do I need an SSD to play GTA 6?
You need internal storage or an official expansion card to play current-gen games. A cheap external USB drive can archive games you are not playing, but you must move them back to play. If you are chronically full, an expansion is the long-term fix.
