GTA 6 storage guide: clear your console before November 19

Nobody knows the exact install size yet — Rockstar hasn't published it. What we do know: recent Rockstar titles and comparable open-world games have landed well north of 100 GB, and launch-day patches never make that smaller. Plan for big, and plan early, because launch week is the worst possible time to discover your SSD is full.

Step 1: check what you actually have (2 minutes)

  • PS5: Settings → Storage. Look at "Games and Apps" — sort by size and be honest with yourself about that 90 GB shooter you haven't opened since March.
  • Xbox Series X|S: My games & apps → Manage storage. Same deal.

If you've got 150+ GB free after cleanup, you're plausibly fine. Skip to step 3.

Step 2: if you're tight — your options, cheapest first

  1. Delete and re-download later. Free. Games you own aren't gone; the download is. The catch: launch week broadband + everyone else re-downloading too.
  2. Cold storage on any old USB drive. PS5 and Xbox can both park (not play) current-gen games on a cheap external HDD and move them back later. This is the best dollars-per-gigabyte move if you shuffle games a few times a year.
  3. A real expansion SSD. The no-compromise option, and the one worth doing right:
  4. PS5 takes standard NVMe M.2 drives — a WD_BLACK SN850X-class NVMe drive is the well-reviewed default; you want 1 TB+ and a heatsink model.
  5. Xbox Series X|S uses the proprietary expansion-card slot — the WD_BLACK C50 expansion card (and Seagate's equivalent) are the only games in town.

Step 3: preload like you mean it

Preorders typically preload a couple of days early. The day preload opens, start it — a 100+ GB download on a mid-tier connection is an overnight job, not an after-work one.

The honest summary

Install-size note: every number here is planning guidance from franchise precedent, not an official spec. When Rockstar publishes the real size, the Discord's news desk will have it within minutes — that's the whole point of the server.
