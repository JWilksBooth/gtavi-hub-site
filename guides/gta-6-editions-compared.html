GTA 6 editions compared

Two editions, a $20 gap, and a preorder bonus with a deadline. Here is the straight version.

The verdict most guides bury

Buy Standard. You can generally upgrade to Ultimate later, which makes the $20 a decision you are allowed to defer until you actually know whether the online mode is your thing. Ultimate's extras are weighted toward online progression and cosmetics — real value if you live in that mode, meaningless if you play the story and drift away.

Buy Ultimate on day one only if you already know you will be deep in the online mode and you would rather not think about it again.

The preorder bonus deadline that matters

Every preorder placed before November 20, 2026 includes the Vintage Vice City Pack, on either edition. That is the one genuine time pressure — not the edition choice.

Physical vs digital

  • Digital preloads automatically, cannot be lost, and is ready at unlock.
  • Physical ships a download code in the box (not the full game on disc), so you still download everything. Early preorder batches were heavily scalped. Buy the box because you want the box, not because you think it is faster.

Before you buy, check your storage

An edition decision is easy; discovering your console is full on launch night is not. Plan for roughly 150 GB free — the storage guide has a calculator and the cheapest ways to make room.

FAQ

How much does GTA 6 cost?
The Standard Edition is $79.99 and the Ultimate Edition is $99.99 at launch on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
Is the GTA 6 Ultimate Edition worth it?
For most players, no — not on day one. Ultimate's value is weighted toward online and cosmetic extras you cannot properly evaluate before release. Buying Standard and upgrading later if the online mode grabs you is the lower-risk path.
Do I still get the Vintage Vice City Pack?
Preorders placed before November 20, 2026 include the Vintage Vice City Pack, on either edition.
Should I preorder GTA 6 physically or digitally?
Digital preloads automatically and cannot be lost. Physical editions ship with a download code in the box rather than the full game on disc, and early batches were heavily scalped. Digital is the simpler choice unless you specifically want the box.
