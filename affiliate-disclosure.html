Affiliate disclosure

Some links on this site are affiliate links. If you buy through one, GTA VI Hub may earn a commission at no extra cost to you.

That is the short version, and it is repeated on every page that carries such a link. The longer version is below, because we would rather over-explain this than have you wonder.

What an affiliate link is

When we recommend a product — an SSD, a headset, a copy of the game — the link may carry a tag identifying us as the referrer. If you buy, the retailer pays us a small percentage. You pay exactly the same price whether you use our link or navigate to the retailer yourself.

How it affects what we write

It doesn't, and here is how we keep that true:

  • We recommend not buying, frequently. Our setup guide's headline advice is that most people need nothing. Our storage guide leads with the free options — delete a game, archive to a drive you already own — before it mentions anything you could buy.
  • We do not write reviews we can't stand behind. Where a product recommendation is generic ("a well-reviewed NVMe drive"), we say so rather than manufacture false specificity.
  • We label estimates. GTA 6's install size is not official. We never dress an inference up as a spec to justify a purchase.
  • Commission rate never determines placement. We do not reorder recommendations by what pays best.

In the Discord

Deal posts in our Discord carry the disclosure line on the post itself, every time — it is added automatically by the software, and there is no code path that can post a deal without it. Deal posts are limited by design: they only go to an opt-in role, never @everyone, and are rate-limited so the channel can't become an ad feed.

What we will not promote

Regardless of what it pays: energy drinks, gambling, casino or betting services, skin betting, loot-box gambling, or untrusted key resellers. Our audience includes minors and those categories have no business in front of them.

Programs

We participate, or have applied to participate, in affiliate programs run by retailers of gaming hardware and software. Where a specific program requires its own disclosure wording, that wording appears on the relevant page.

Questions

Ask in the Discord or email contact@ (set CONTACT_EMAIL in .env). If you think a recommendation on this site is not honest, tell us — we would genuinely rather fix it.
