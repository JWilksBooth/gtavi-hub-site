Privacy policy

Short version: this website sets no analytics cookies, runs no trackers, and collects nothing about you. The longer version follows, including the parts we don't control.

This website

GTA VI Hub is a static site. There is no account system, no login, no comment system, no newsletter signup, and no contact form.

  • No analytics. We run no Google Analytics, no tag manager, no pixel, no heatmap tool.
  • No advertising networks. No ad scripts run here.
  • No cookies set by us. The interactive tools (storage calculator, countdown, crew-name generator) run entirely in your browser and store nothing.
  • No fingerprinting, no session recording, no third-party fonts or scripts.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which as a hosting provider may log standard request data such as IP address for security and abuse prevention. That is GitHub's processing, not ours, and we do not receive or access it.

Affiliate links

Some outbound links are affiliate links (see our affiliate disclosure). When you click one, the destination retailer or its affiliate network will typically set a cookie so a resulting purchase can be attributed to us. That is the retailer's processing under their own privacy policy — we receive only aggregate, anonymous commission reporting. We never receive your name, address, payment details, or what you bought.

Some affiliate links pass through a redirect on our own infrastructure to count clicks. That counter records only the link that was clicked and the date. It does not record IP addresses, does not set cookies, and cannot identify you.

The Discord community

Discord is a separate service with its own privacy policy. If you join our server, our bot processes:

  • Your Discord user ID, join date, and roles — to grant roles and run community features.
  • A count of your messages — for activity levels and the free "OG" role. We store the count, not the content.
  • Crew membership, if you use the crew-finder.

We do not read, store, or analyse the content of your messages. We do not build advertising profiles. We do not sell or share any of it. If you leave the server, ask and we will delete your records.

Supporters

If you buy the Founding 100 badge or tip, payment is processed entirely by Ko-fi and its payment providers. We never see or store card details. We receive the transaction ID, the name or note you chose to provide, and the amount — used only to grant the role and credit you on the founders wall.

Children

The community is intended for ages 13+, consistent with Discord's own minimum age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided information, contact us and we will remove it.

Your rights

Because we collect almost nothing, there is usually nothing to export or erase. If you have been in the Discord and want your records deleted, ask — we will do it and confirm.
